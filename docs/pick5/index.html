Pick 4 → Pick 5 triggers

every Pick 4 triple in any state, and whether that triple showed up in a Pick 5 within 7 days
What 2+ years of draws say
A trigger is any Pick 4 draw with three or four of the same digit (like 7774). It "came" if a Pick 5 draw in any of the 10 Pick 5 states had three or more of that digit, in any order, in the 7 days after. "Random" is how often three of a given digit show up anyway: about 1 Pick 5 draw in 117. "Copy 55" copies the full box cover for that digit (like 77700 through 77799), ready to paste into Deflate 5. The live count starts Oct 14, 2026, so it tracks new triples going forward.

A Pick 5 draw has three or more of a given digit (like 88800 through 88899) about 1 time in 117, or 0.86%. Long waits came slightly more often than chance, but shuffling each state's draws into random order matched or beat 1.09 about 1 time in 15, and 1.39 about 1 time in 18, so neither is proven. Triples did not travel to other states more than chance; a triple shows up somewhere in the 10 states in about 6 of every 10 weeks anyway. The two live tests count only from Oct 14, 2026 forward. 10-way sequence watch

every 10-way hit, and its associated boxes a week before and a week after, all states
For each 10-way number (three of one digit and two of another, like 80808), its associated boxes are every box your methods make from it: Endeavor, the four other workbook rundowns, the mirror, the 814 rundown and the string projection (about 55–60 boxes). "Before" counts how many of those came in any state in the 7 days before the hit; "After" counts the 7 days after. "Random" is how many random boxes of the same types would come in the same number of draws. Weeks still in progress show as partial and are left out of the totals until they finish. Tap a row to see which boxes came.

Methods tested

every Pick 5 method we could code, on 17,647 real draws
Each method was fed a state's last draw and graded on that state's next draw in the same slot, Jan 2024 – Oct 12, 2026 (Louisiana from 2021). "Chance" is how many hits picking that many numbers at random would get. A ratio near 1.00 means the method did what random picks do. Every bet returns about 50¢ per $1 on average whichever numbers are played.
